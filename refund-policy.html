Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Our services are delivered and experiential in nature — campaign production, coordination and creator payments happen on a rolling basis. Please read this policy carefully before you engage us. By agreeing to a service contract or using our Services you accept the terms below.

1. Generally, no refunds

Once a service fee (for example, a campaign fee) is paid, it is non-refundable in line with the delivered nature of the work, unless covered by the cases below. Work already produced, coordinated or delivered cannot be "returned".

2. When we do refund

  • Duplicate payments: if you are charged twice for the same service, we refund the duplicate in full.
  • Failed or incorrect payments: if a payment fails but an amount was debited, the amount is auto-reversed by the payment gateway; if not, we initiate a full refund.
  • Payment made to the wrong item: if you paid for a service you did not intend to, we transfer the amount to the correct item or refund it if we cannot adjust it, provided you contact us within 7 days.
  • Service cancellation: if we cancel a campaign or service entirely, we refund the unearned portion of the amount paid.
  • Non-delivery: if we fail to deliver the agreed service within the contract's timeline, you may request a refund of the relevant portion.

3. Creator payments & the network

Joining and leaving the creator network is always free. Creator payments follow each campaign's agreed terms and are released once deliverables are accepted. Payment disputes are resolved on the record — against the agreed contract, not on sentiment.

4. How to request a refund

  • Send the request through the campaign inquiry form with the subject "refund", including your name, payment reference/transaction ID and the reason for the request.
  • Send the request within 7 days of the payment.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days of approval. For UPI and card payments, the timeline also depends on the bank or gateway.

5. Chargebacks

We encourage you to contact us first before initiating a chargeback with your bank. A chargeback raised without contacting us may result in the termination of our engagement and a record with the payment gateway.

Questions? Send them through the campaign inquiry form and we’ll help — we optimise for trust, not disputes.
