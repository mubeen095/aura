Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aura Sphere collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use our website, apply to the creator network, or send us a brand inquiry ("the Services").

1. Information we collect

  • Creator applications: name, email, phone, city, primary category, content languages, platform handles, follower counts, average views, audience information, past collaborations, portfolio links and the reasons you provide for joining.
  • Brand inquiries: brand name, contact person, business email, phone, campaign goal, budget band and a message.
  • Usage information: pages visited, device type, browser and interactions on our website, collected through analytics cookies and similar technologies.
  • Campaign data: deliverables, contracts and payment records created while campaigns run.

2. How we use your information

  • To review creator applications and build a network that matches campaigns honestly.
  • To respond to brand inquiries and deliver contracted campaign services.
  • To manage campaigns, process creator payments and issue receipts.
  • To improve our website and services, and to contact you about relevant opportunities you have opted into.

3. Legal basis

We process your information where necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the Services), to comply with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Services. Where required, we rely on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

4. Sharing your information

  • Service providers: payment processors, hosting and analytics providers, and font/CDN services. They process data only for the services they provide to us.
  • Campaign partners: where a campaign requires it, creator profile and delivery information is shared with the brand and its agencies under contract.
  • Legal compliance: where required by law, court order or government authority.
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the new entity.

We never sell your personal information.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your network membership or engagement continues, and for a reasonable period afterwards to comply with tax, legal and campaign-record obligations.

6. Your rights

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, mention it in a creator application or brand inquiry and we'll act on it.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption in transit. No method of transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with the updated date shown above.

11. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, use the inquiry form on the brands page, or mention it in a creator application.
