BRAND FAQS

ASK US ANYTHING.

How do you select creators?

We look at audience relevance, content, category, language, geography, engagement and campaign requirements.

Can you run campaigns across multiple Indian languages?

Yes — campaigns can be structured around regional audiences and language requirements based on available creator supply.

What types of campaigns do you manage?

Brand awareness, product launches, UGC, regional campaigns, performance campaigns and long-term creator partnerships.

Do you handle creator negotiation?

Yes. Campaign communication, commercial discussions and deliverables can be managed through AuraSphere.

Do you handle content approvals and deadlines?

Yes. AuraSphere can coordinate creator briefs, approvals, revisions and campaign timelines.

Can you help with UGC?

Yes — creator-generated content can be incorporated into campaigns depending on the brief and usage requirements.

How quickly can a campaign start?

Campaign timelines depend on the scope, creator requirements and approvals. Share your goal and we'll plan a realistic timeline.

What information do you need to start a campaign?

Campaign objective, target audience, category, geography, language, deliverables, timeline and budget are useful starting points.
